## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-16

### Added

- Bounded, on-demand scanning of persisted Pi sessions.
- Redacted summary and timeline reports for tool, model, Bash, subagent, and extension failures.
- Current-session and all-session scopes.
- `report`, `new`, `show`, and `compare` diagnostic actions.
- Filters for kind, source, project, time window, and per-request noise exclusions.
- Deterministic recovery hints for common failure patterns.
- Strict privacy, input-budget, output-budget, cancellation, and compatibility tests.
